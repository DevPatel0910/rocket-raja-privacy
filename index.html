Privacy Policy

Rocket Raja: Fireworks Tycoon
Android game published by Diwali Games

Gameplay and local storage

The game saves your shop name, progress, rewards and settings on your device. This version does not require or create a game account. It does not sync your shop or progress to the cloud or process in-game purchases. To erase your local progress, clear the game's storage in Android settings. This also clears your locally saved preferences and may be irreversible.

Optional rewarded ads

The game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage ad privacy choices and offer rewarded videos. The game prepares these services so an ad can be available when you choose to watch. Google may collect and receive your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, ad and app interactions, performance and diagnostic data, and identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information to provide and measure advertising, operate and improve its services, and prevent fraud. The information sent by Google's mobile ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

When available, Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings lets you change your ad choices. You can also manage or delete the advertising ID in your Android privacy settings. Google explains how it handles this information and how you can manage it in its policy for sites and apps that use Google services and its Privacy Policy.

Service and support data

Firebase libraries are present for platform services. They may process installation identifiers and service metadata. The game disables Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection. Google Play can provide us with aggregate testing, crash and performance reports. Google describes its service handling and retention in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

If you email us, we receive your address and what you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request. We retain it for as long as needed to resolve the request and to meet applicable legal or security obligations. Google retains data it processes under the policies linked above.
